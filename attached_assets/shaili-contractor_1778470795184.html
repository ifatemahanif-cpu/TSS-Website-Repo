Headout · Content Leadership

Leading two European markets to the top of search — and the top of mind

I led the content teams for Headout's France and Italy-Switzerland markets — building not just pages, but an entire content infrastructure that did two jobs at once: rank on Google and turn readers into bookers. Every piece of content had to earn its place twice over.

The France market started from zero. A new domain, new city micro-brands, new editorial standards — and Page 1 rankings across key cities within three months of launch. Italy and Switzerland followed the same model: consistent Page 1 presence, content built to answer real questions, and revenue-linked pages that moved people from discovery to booking.

FirstCry · IntelliEducation · Brand Architecture

Building an entire early learning brand — from the first page to four verticals

IntelliEducation didn't exist yet. I was there before the first page was written — helping shape the structure, the voice, and the content strategy that would branch into four sub-brands.

IntelliBaby — strategy and content across 50 products for India's youngest learners.

IntelliKits — Montessori-inspired, Multiple Intelligence-based monthly subscription kits for ages 2–6. Four kits a month, 50+ kits developed from concept to copy.

Intelliskills — educational toys, puzzles, and books built around STEAM learning and early childhood development. Led content across 80+ products.

Alongside all of this, built the IntelliEducation editorial blog — expert-backed, interview-led, written for parents and educators who needed to trust what they were reading.

Little Black Book · Editorial Leadership

Growing a city's content output 15x — without losing the voice that made it matter

As AGM and Editor for LBB Mumbai, I led a team of 7 across content, strategy, and operations. The challenge was never just volume — it was editorial quality at scale. We grew content output 15x and page traffic 40%.

I also managed LBB Delhi for a stint, and worked directly with the founder to keep the LBB homepage and app content fresh and relevant daily — using data to shape what surfaced, when, and why. Built content IPs that gave the platform repeatable formats with longevity, not just one-off pieces. Managed push notifications and India-wide content strategy, shaping how LBB showed up for readers across cities.

STEM Learning · Editorial

Making learning content worth reading — for school kids and for donors

STEM Learning came with two distinct briefs. The first: the Save for Change fundraiser website, built alongside UX and UI designers to move donors and tell the story of what happens when schools get what they need. Every section had to earn its place — clarity and emotion working together.

The second was editorial: serving as the voice layer across school magazines for grades 5–8, and manuals on AI and technology. The job was to make complex ideas genuinely engaging for young readers — without flattening them. Over 50 magazines and publications shaped across the programme.

About me

Beyond the work.

I'm a trained journalist who has spent 18 years working at the intersection of brand strategy, content strategy, and editorial leadership. Three things most people treat as separate disciplines — and that I've always seen as the same conversation.

Words are never just words. They're the strategy made visible. I've spent my career helping brands understand the difference between saying something and meaning it.

I started in advertising — writing for General Mills, Axis Bank, Google Pixel, Heinz, and Bharti AXA, among others. Moved into editorial, then spent years building early learning brands from scratch, then led content teams across travel, media, and education. The industries changed. The questions didn't: What is this brand actually saying? Is the content doing the work it needs to do? And does it earn the trust it's asking for?

Outside work, I garden obsessively, write stories for children, and collect graphic novels with the seriousness of someone building an archive. Bone by Jeff Smith is my favourite. Middlesex by Jeffrey Eugenides is one of the finest novels written. I also give time to cleaning up my city — because some things need more than a good narrative to fix them.
