## §7 FAQ (rewritten around what founders actually ask)

Header: **Questions we get asked.**

**I don't know what my brand should say. Is that a problem?**
That is the job. Most founders can describe what they do in conversation and freeze when it has to go on a page. The call is where we find the words, and then we write them.

**How much of my time will this take?**
About four hours total: a thirty-minute call, one drop of photos and information, and two rounds of feedback. We do not need you in a weekly meeting.

**What do I need to have ready?**
Photos, any product or service detail, and whatever brand material exists. If you have nothing written, that is fine, and it is the usual case.

**Who actually writes it?**
The four of us. There are no juniors and nobody is being handed your brand after the call.

**What if I don't like the first version?**
Two rounds of revisions per page are included. If something is off, it is usually the positioning rather than the design, and that conversation happens before anything is built.

**Can I edit the site myself afterwards?**
Not without a developer. This build has no CMS, which is what keeps it fast and keeps the price flat. Text changes come back to us or to any developer you like.

**Why only five?**
Because five is what four people can write and build properly in a month while running client work. When they are gone, the next opening is September.

**What happens if I need more pages, or a store, later?**
Both are separate conversations at a separate price. Say it on the call and we will scope it honestly.

**Is ₹75,000 the whole number?**
Yes, for everything in the You Get column. Your domain and hosting stay in your name and typically cost nothing on a free tier.
