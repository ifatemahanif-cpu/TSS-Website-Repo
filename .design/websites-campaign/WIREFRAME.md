12 Aug. Supersedes the v1 page structure and the IA doc. Price is now **₹79,000** (79th year of independence — ties the "Freedom from websites" hero to Independence Day). Fatema supplies copy per section; build follows this structure exactly. Design phase uses the design skills stack (what Fatema calls "G-Stack" = design-taste-frontend et al.).

## Page structure

**1. HERO** — the whole pitch, no scroll needed
- Eyebrow: AUGUST · FIVE SLOTS
- Headline: autocomplete typewriter (locked from v1)
- Subtext: one written paragraph that WEAVES IN the facts — ₹79,000 flat, up to 5 pages, copy + build, live in 10 working days, five brands only. No separate facts strip.
- CTA: Book my slot → form
- Deadline line under CTA (applications close 31 August)
- Job: hero must work hard enough that no problem section is needed.

**2. WHY US** — credibility + proof, one section
- "The website is the easy part." credibility copy (45+ years) with the brands ticker inside the section as proof
- Job: believe we can write.

**3. THE OFFER** — get / don't-do tick table
- Included vs not-included, price line beneath
- Job: scope clarity on the page; the legal detail lives in the T&C doc.

**4. FORM** — "Book my slot."
- 14 questions, terms checkbox with the T&C hyperlinked in the checkbox label
- Job: apply without a call.

**5. FAQ** — questions founders actually ask

**6. FOOTER**
- T&C is a LINK here (and from the form checkbox) — not an on-page appendix.

## T&C — separate linked document (own page, not on the LP)

Legally written: inclusions, exclusions, and an explicit clause that any extra services beyond scope can be scoped at a separate charge, discussed on the call. Also carries payment terms (the ₹25,000 slot deposit + balance at go-live), revision rounds, the silence window, asset-delay clause.
